A tiny program that writes exactly one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` —
an absurd clause in perfect legal register: by clicking "I Agree," you
grant the Company a perpetual, non-exclusive license to **the left sock
of any pair you wear on a Tuesday**, collectable within thirty (30) days
of written notice. The line was drafted with an OpenCode free-model round,
then won a criteria-ordered tournament against the judge's published
criteria (a clause that could hide in a terms-of-service agreement, funny
on one reading, boilerplate register until the absurdity hits, original
and specific, one clean tight sentence), beating the sneeze-ownership
transfer and the houseplant duty. Output is byte-exact: 190 UTF-8 bytes,
no trailing newline. Run it with `python3 main.py`.
